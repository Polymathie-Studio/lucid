LUCID · the shipped core, inlined

One concept model, rendered by the module itself

This page inlines the actual vanilla core (lucid.js) and its stylesheet, unchanged, then registers a small dictionary. Everything you see is the shipped binding, not a bespoke renderer. Hover a marked term for its gloss, click to open the explainer, detail, visual, and relations, then Open the source.

The first thing a founder names is their operating shape, because it decides what counts as evidence. Under it sits the demand signal, which a make-a-market venture reads the opposite way an incumbent does, and which carries a small visual drawn by a host-supplied renderer. A term with no deeper layers, like runway, still glosses on hover but does not open.

The relation chips are clickable (the host wired onSelect to scroll to the related term). The bar chart proves the type versus instance split: the dictionary holds only the spec, and the page supplied the numbers through getData. Swap the tiny inline renderer for Vega-Lite and nothing else changes.
